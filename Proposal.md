### Phase 2 — Google Play Closed Testing (Required, Included in Fixed Price)

Google now requires new apps to complete a **closed testing period before going live on the Play Store**:

- Recruit and manage **at least 12 real test accounts**
- Run the closed test for a **minimum of 14 days**
- Collect tester feedback, fix reported issues, and re-upload updated builds during that period
- Once the 14-day requirement is met and Google approves, promote the app to production

This is mandatory for all new Google Play developer accounts and adds approximately 2–3 weeks to the Android timeline.

### Phase 3 — Improvements (Hourly)
