Frequently Asked Questions

What is Vet School App?

Vet School App helps aspiring veterinarians compare every accredited U.S. veterinary school in one place. You can explore tuition, admissions requirements, acceptance rates, prerequisites, NAVLE outcomes, specialties, and deadlines — all through a simple, easy-to-use interface.

How do I compare schools?

Tap any school to view its full profile, then use the compare feature to view two or more schools side by side. You can filter by tuition, acceptance rate, location, and more.

How do I track my application?

Use the Admissions Tracker to add schools to your list, set deadline reminders, and monitor your application progress. Enable notifications to receive timely reminders.

How does school matching work?

The personalized matching feature asks about your GPA, experience, location preferences, and interests, then surfaces schools that are a strong fit for your profile.

The app isn't loading data — what should I do?

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Force-close the app and reopen it.
  • Make sure you have the latest version installed from the App Store or Google Play.
